# Yak Herding
Most projects I work on use the "yaks" plugin, either checked in or .gitignore'd for shared
projects. Always remember the following:
- If yaks are installed in the current working dir, *always* update yaks as you work. Don't start
  without 'shaving' a yak; don't finish without marking them 'shorn'.
- Yaks are a good place to keep notes as you work. You can always refer back to your notes to recall
  the associated status.
